CardFinder AI: Card Scanner

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information CardFinder AI: Card Scanner ("CardFinder AI", "the app") handles and why. CardFinder AI is developed by Mehmet Emin Yazıcıoğlu, an individual developer ("we", "us").

The short version

  • No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your collection stays on your iPhone.
  • Card photos you scan are sent to our server and an AI service only to identify or grade the card, and are not stored.
  • Our server keeps a random anonymous ID and how many scans you've used. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Delete my data.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your collection: cards, quantities, folders, purchase prices, notes and any photos you save with a card.
  • Settings such as your currency, card types and onboarding progress.

This data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, which Apple and you control. Deleting the app, or Settings → Delete my data, removes it.

Information our server handles

The app uses a backend hosted on Supabase.

  • Anonymous ID: when you first open the app, it creates a random account ID with no name, e-mail or phone number. It lets us count your free scans.
  • Usage counts: how many scans and AI Pre-Grades you've used, and when you last scanned.
  • Subscription status: whether you have Premium, which plan and when it ends, so the server can unlock Premium features.
  • Abuse protection: short-lived request counters keyed by your anonymous ID and IP address, used only to stop abuse. They're deleted within about a day.

Card photos and AI

When you scan a card or use AI Pre-Grade, the photo is sent to our server and then to Google's Gemini API (Google LLC) to identify the card or estimate its condition. Photos are processed to answer that request and are not stored on our server. Google processes them under its Gemini API terms as a service provider. Please photograph only the card, not people or documents.

Prices

To show values, our server looks up the card's name, set and number in public catalogs and marketplaces (TCGdex, Scryfall, YGOPRODeck, Cardmarket, TCGplayer and eBay). Only card details are sent, never information about you.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, e-mail, payment details or Apple Account. We only learn whether a subscription is active.

What we don't do

  • We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't show ads.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

CardFinder AI is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete my data in the app. This deletes your collection on the device and your anonymous ID, usage counts and subscription record on our server. Purchases stay with your Apple Account and come back with Restore Purchases. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) you may have further rights to access or delete personal data; contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
