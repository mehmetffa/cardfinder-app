CardFinder AI identifies trading cards from a photo and shows what they're worth: Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh! and sports cards. The answers below cover the most common questions. Still stuck? E-mail us at social.cardscanner@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can.

Scanning

How do I get the best scan?

Take the card out of its sleeve or top loader if you can, lay it flat on a plain surface in even light and fill the frame with it. Avoid glare from lamps or windows on holo cards.

The app found the wrong card.

Check "Other matches" below the result first. If the right card isn't there, tap "Not right? Edit" and fix the name, set or number; the app searches again with your changes.

Which cards are supported?

Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering and Yu-Gi-Oh! cards are matched against full catalogs. Sports cards (baseball, basketball, football, soccer and more) are identified by the AI and valued from current eBay listings.

Does scanning work offline?

No. Identifying a card and getting prices need an internet connection. Your collection is stored on your iPhone, so you can browse it offline.

Prices

Where do the prices come from?

From marketplace data: Cardmarket and TCGplayer for Pokémon, Magic and Yu-Gi-Oh!, and current eBay listings for sports cards. Each price shows its source and when it was last updated. Prices in other currencies are converted at the day's exchange rate.

Why are there several prices for one card?

Different marketplaces and printings (for example holo, reverse holo or 1st Edition) sell for different amounts. The big number is the market that fits your currency best; the rows below show the others.

Are the prices exact?

No. They're estimates of what cards like yours sell for, not an offer to buy and not financial advice. The condition of your card can change its value a lot.

AI Pre-Grade

What is AI Pre-Grade?

An estimate of the grade a grading company might give your card, based on centering, corners, edges and surface in your photos, plus whether grading is likely to pay off after the fee. It's an AI estimate, not an official grade, and CardFinder AI isn't affiliated with PSA or any grading company.

Subscriptions

What's free and what's in Premium?

Scanning and identifying cards is free, and Magic: The Gathering prices are always free. Premium unlocks raw and graded values for every card, AI Pre-Grade, unlimited scans and an unlimited collection with its total value.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open CardFinder AI → Settings → Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → CardFinder AI, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid being charged again. You keep Premium until the end of the period.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.
